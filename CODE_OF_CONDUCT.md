# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.
Participants are expected to:

- be considerate and constructive;
- assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements;
- focus feedback on the work, not on people;
- respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and communication styles;
- keep public and private project information confidential.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
sexualized language or attention, threats, doxxing, trolling, and deliberately
disruptive conduct.

## Enforcement

Please report problems to the repository maintainers through the private
contact method listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), or contact a maintainer
directly through their verified GitHub profile. Reports are handled
confidentially where possible. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues,
restrict participation, or take other action needed to keep the community safe.

This code of conduct applies in project repositories, issue trackers, pull
requests, discussions, and official project spaces.
